Privacy Policy for Full Screen Clock

Effective Date: July 28, 2026  |  Last Updated: July 28, 2026

Summary of Our Policy

Full Screen Clock is committed to user privacy. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, location information, or usage statistics.

1. Information Collection and Use

Full Screen Clock does not collect or store any personal information. All features operate entirely on your device locally without sending data to external servers.

2. Device Status & Battery Access

The application reads battery status directly on your local device solely to display the current battery percentage on the screen while the clock is active.

  • Battery status data is processed transiently in memory on the device.
  • No battery data or telemetry is recorded, stored permanently, or transmitted across the network.
  • No other system information, device identifiers, or diagnostics are collected.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

This application does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Firebase), or tracking services. No data is shared with any third parties.

4. Permissions Used

The application requests only minimal system privileges necessary for its core clock and display functions:

  • Battery Status Access: Used exclusively to show real-time charge levels on screen.

5. Children's Privacy

Full Screen Clock does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. It complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

6. Security

Since the app does not collect, transmit, or store any user data, your personal information is completely secure from network interception or unauthorized server access.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
